Our mission

Help every organization adopt AI with confidence, without slowing its people down.

AI tools arrive faster than policies can keep up. Most organizations can’t say which tools their people use, what data goes into them, or who signed off. GovernBright closes that gap: it makes AI use visible, gives each decision an owner, and keeps a record you can show an auditor, a customer or a regulator.

Visibility

A single register of every AI system, plus shadow-AI discovery from sign-in and expense data, so nothing hides in the gaps.

Accountability

Named owners, a versioned AI use policy with per-person sign-off, and an approval path for every new tool.

Evidence

Append-only audit records and risk assessments whose controls cite the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, GDPR and ISO/IEC 42001.

People first

A clear yes-or-no in place of quiet workarounds: employees ask, admins decide, and approved seats are provisioned automatically.
